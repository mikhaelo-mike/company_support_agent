TECHNOVA COMPANY KNOWLEDGE BASE

====================================
1. COMPANY OVERVIEW
====================================

TechNova is an electronics company that sells
laptops, smartphones, tablets, and accessories.

Our mission is to provide quality electronic
products and reliable customer service.


====================================
2. DELIVERY POLICY
====================================

Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days.

Express delivery takes 1-2 business days.

Customers receive free delivery for orders
above $100.

Orders below $100 incur a delivery fee of $5.

Customers receive a tracking number after
their orders have been dispatched.


====================================
3. RETURN AND REFUND POLICY
====================================

Customers can return products within 7 days
of receiving their orders.

Products must be returned in their original
condition and packaging.

Refunds are processed within 5-7 business
days after the returned product is inspected.


====================================
4. ORDER CANCELLATION POLICY
====================================

Customers can cancel orders before they
are dispatched.

Once an order has been dispatched,
customers cannot cancel it.

Customers should contact customer support
for cancellation assistance.


====================================
5. DAMAGED PRODUCTS POLICY
====================================

Customers must report damaged products
within 48 hours of delivery.

Customers should provide photographs
showing the damage.

TechNova will arrange a replacement or
refund after reviewing the complaint.


====================================
6. CUSTOMER SUPPORT
====================================

Email: support@technova.com

Working hours:
Monday to Friday, 8 AM - 5 PM.

Customers can contact the support team
for assistance with orders, refunds,
returns, and delivery questions.
